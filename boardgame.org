      laying out contents onto surrounding free cells (boardgame-container-layout-

      hand).

      heading char or fallback *), adds object to active board, and removes the subtree  
      from source.
      • Take command (boardgame-hand-take): transfers board objects into active hand;    
      specialized to take the top contained item when the cursor is over a container or  
      stack without taking or destroying the container/stack itself.
